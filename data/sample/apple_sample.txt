Apple Inc. — Sample Filing Excerpt (for offline testing only)

Business Overview:
Apple Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers,
tablets, wearables, and accessories, and sells a variety of related services.
The Company's products include iPhone, Mac, iPad, and wearables such as
AirPods and Apple Watch.

Risk Factors:
The Company's business, reputation, results of operations, and financial
condition can be affected by global and regional economic conditions,
including inflation, interest rate fluctuations, and currency exchange rate
volatility. The Company faces substantial competition in all markets in
which it operates, and this competition could result in reduced margins.

Revenue Recognition:
Net sales disaggregated by significant products and services for fiscal year
2023 included iPhone, Mac, iPad, Wearables/Home/Accessories, and Services.
Services revenue includes advertising, AppleCare, cloud services, digital
content, and payment services, and has grown as a share of total revenue.

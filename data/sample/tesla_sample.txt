Tesla, Inc. — Sample Filing Excerpt (for offline testing only)

Business Overview:
Tesla designs, develops, manufactures, sells, and leases electric vehicles,
and energy generation and storage systems. Tesla also offers vehicle service
and sells automotive regulatory credits.

Manufacturing:
The Company operates vehicle manufacturing facilities, including Gigafactory
Texas, Gigafactory Shanghai, and Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg, in addition
to its original facility in Fremont, California.

Risk Factors:
Tesla has experienced rapid growth, which has placed, and may continue to
place, significant demands on its operational, financial, and management
infrastructure. Delays in production ramp of new vehicle models could
adversely affect the Company's business and financial condition.
